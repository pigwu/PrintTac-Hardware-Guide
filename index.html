FINGERTIP ASSEMBLY

Assemble the tactile fingertips

  1. Confirm the magnet layout

    Follow the matching CAD for pocket positions, magnet count, and polarity. Keep both fingers consistent with the reference sensing configuration. Confirm polarity before permanently securing the magnets.

  2. Mount the sensor board and flexure

    Keep the magnetometer board fixed to the base while the magnets move with the contact plate. Check that tightening does not bend the board or constrain the flexure. Route cables clear of moving parts.

  3. Attach the replaceable contact tip

    Use the fasteners specified by the design. Preserve the internal magnetic sensing stack when replacing a tip. Check the unloaded signal and elastic return after assembly and each replacement.

  4. Validate one finger, then assemble the pair

    Gently touch the tip and inspect all five chips. Signals should respond to contact and return to a stable unloaded baseline. Once both fingers pass, mount them on the gripper and secure the phone and cables.

GRIPPER CALIBRATION

Calibrate the gripper aperture

In ArUco Gripper, use two measured jaw openings to calibrate the width.

Marker dictionaryDICT_4X4_50
Marker IDs0 and 1
Black outer edge16 mm
Open PrintTac Capture

Complete the desktop setup, then run this from the repository root. Replace PHONE_IP with your iPhone's IP address.

Open the monitor · Windows PowerShell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe printtac.py monitor --phone-ip PHONE_IP

On macOS, replace .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe with ./.venv/bin/python.

  1. Check markers and depth

    Mount one marker on each jaw at similar height and depth, with camera X along the opening direction. Confirm both are visible in Live Monitor → 0.5× Ultra-wide (ArUco). In ArUco Gripper, check the settings below, click Use Current Depth and Apply, and wait for Measuring each frame.

  2. Measure and capture both endpoints

    Measure the minimum and maximum gaps between the inner contact surfaces with calipers. Enter them in Actual opening (caliper), in mm. At the minimum opening, hold still for one second and click Capture Minimum; repeat at the maximum with Capture Maximum. Keep both markers visible.

  3. Save and check

    Click Finish: Save and Apply Calibration. Compare Calibrated opening with caliper measurements at a few intermediate positions. Recalibrate after moving the markers, remounting the phone, or changing the contact tips.

Optional: Use repeated open/close cycles

Enable Optional: Record open/close cycles for robust calibration and click Start Recording. Move between the measured endpoints for at least Minimum complete cycles (default: 5). Click Finish and Calculate, then Save and Apply.
